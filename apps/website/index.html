CORE APP FEATURES

Explore the mechanics that turn cardio workouts into high-stakes gaming battles.

GPS Territory Mapping

Your real-world coordinates slice the map into tactical hexagonal zones. Cross boundaries during runs to claim ownership.

Real-time PvP Clashes

Running into another player's territory instantly triggers in-app alerts. Outspeed them to invade and take their sector.
