FEATURES & IN-APP MECHANICS

A deep look at how StrideClash bridges physical movement with tactical virtual warfare.

GPS Hex Grid Mapping

Our backend maps your live running track onto hexagonal sectors of the city. Cross into a sector during a workout to paint it your color and claim ownership.

Live PvP Turf Battles

Enter an opponent's claimed zone to trigger a PvP warning popup. To conquer their sector, you must complete your session with a higher speed or total distance than their record.

Offline Guard Alerts

Rest easy. If a player invades your sector while you are offline, our database token fallback sends instant push notifications to your lock screen so you can plan your counterattack.

Arena Shop & Economy

Workout metrics are converted into Arena Coins. Spend your earnings in the shop to purchase and unlock custom avatars and premium companion tags.
